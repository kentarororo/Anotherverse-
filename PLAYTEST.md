# First Playable Slice — Human Gate

Open the deployed [100-paragraph review harness](https://kentarororo.github.io/Anotherverse-/?review=corpus)
for the separate corpus read-aloud gate. It tracks the required 95 grammatically natural and 85
contextually coherent thresholds in local browser storage.

Start a New Campaign on the deployed schema-13 build, choose one starting hero, and play through
Turn 20. Record the seed and any turn where the cause, choice, or result is unclear.

## Required observations

- Can you explain what makes each hero mechanically distinct?
- Does the chosen lead appear alone at first, with the other two companions joining naturally in
  Chapters 1 and 2?
- Before each operation, can you identify one useful formation, stance, or priority change?
- Can you identify all five combatants, the active actor and target, HP change or status, and the
  selected plan without opening the exact battle log?
- Can you identify which earlier fact or choice caused each personal, discovery, rival, or social
  situation?
- Can you explain what each hero is progressing toward and spend at least one training point per
  hero?
- Can you explain why each battle resolved as it did using the expandable mechanics?
- Win a battle, inspect all three named monster materials in the Forge, fuse them, equip the relic,
  and confirm its named enemy counter appears in a later forecast and combat event.
- Open Character, Inventory, Bestiary, World, and Logs drawers using keyboard navigation; verify
  Escape closes them and focus returns to the opener.
- Confirm Bestiary confidence rises after a battle; Rations, Coin, Dust, Gear, and any new drop are
  visible in the header; and the squad reaches a new Path rank.
- Confirm Chapter 3 is the first full-trio battle and victory awards one named material per enemy,
  Coin, and relic dust.
- Reload after Turn 10 and confirm Turn 11 produces the expected situation and outcome.

## Pass criteria

All five questions above have clear answers; no exact scenario fingerprint repeats before Turn 20;
no unresolved text slot or contradictory situation appears; and no save, layout, or navigation
failure prevents continuing.
