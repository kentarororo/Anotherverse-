# System GDD — Progression & Economy

## Intent

Every reward should create a stronger build or a meaningful near-term choice.

## Power model

Power comes from four legible layers:

1. **Level:** reliable base-stat growth after earned XP.
2. **Equipment:** encounter-facing bonuses and counters.
3. **Techniques:** role expression and conditional tactical power.
4. **Mythic Awakening:** personal progression earned by repeated character-defining choices, never random loot.

Level-up must change combat-derived values. Awakening stage is not a cosmetic label.

## Slice resources

| Resource          | Sources                        | Required sinks                                   | Rule                                                 |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Rations           | Start, rescue rewards          | Recover HP/readiness; prepare a travel advantage | A spend must matter in the next battle.              |
| Coin              | Battle and quest rewards       | Buy, repair, or reveal a fusion hint             | At least one offer after Turns 2, 4, and 5.          |
| Relic Dust        | Elite/boss rewards, duplicates | Fuse or improve equipment                        | Keep only if usable by Turn 4.                       |
| Monster materials | Named enemy drops              | Weighted fusion recipes                          | Material name must imply origin and likely property. |

Danger remains only if implementation gives it an encounter modifier and visible thresholds; otherwise it is removed from the player HUD. Renown and rank remain progression summaries only when they unlock content in the slice.

## Loot and ownership

- Each combat awards one highlighted reward; avoid undifferentiated bundles.
- The slice contains at least twelve items across offence, defence, support, and counter utility.
- An item can be equipped by only one hero.
- New, improved, and currently useful items are visibly marked.
- Reward preview states category and purpose, not guaranteed hidden identity.

## Fusion

Fusion is bounded discovery, not arbitrary crafting. Two materials plus optional Relic Dust select from a weighted, world-compatible recipe table. The same seed, inputs, and unlocked knowledge produce the same result. A preview shows likely tags; discovered combinations become permanent recipe knowledge.

## Readiness and recovery

Readiness modifies technique access or effectiveness at clear thresholds and is reduced by demanding battles. Rations, rest choices, and support skills restore it. If readiness cannot affect the following encounter, do not show it.

## Balance gates

- A level, item upgrade, or Awakening stage creates at least a 10% measurable improvement in its intended combat dimension.
- Every currency has a sink reachable within two turns of first acquisition.
- No required progression depends on a rare random drop.
- A player reaches the boss with at least two materially different viable builds.
- Inventory never contains two equipped owners for one item ID.
